1 · Snow Simulation Explorations Icon-Glyph Alignment

In sky.js today, drawSnow() paints 70 plain white circles (cr.arc), which read as generic white dots and collide visually with hail pellets and snow grains. Meanwhile, painter.js (snowFlakes) and the icon set use a crisp six-spoke asterisk glyph (#flake) with round caps and a lazy rocking tumble. Below are four ways to bring that exact glyph language into the animated sky backdrop:

0 · Current sky.js (Dots)

Baseline
Current · 70 plain circles Icon vs Sky mismatch

What it does: 70 uniform white discs (r = 1.8–3.6) at 0.85 alpha with a single-frequency horizontal sine wave. Lacks crystalline identity and rotational life; indistinguishable from small hailstones.

A · Pure 6-Spoke Icon Glyphs

1:1 Icon Glyph
Mock A · All 6-spoke #flake glyphs Exact 6-spoke glyph

What it does: Every flake uses the exact 6-spoke round-capped asterisk from painter.js (3 crossed lines at 60°), rocking lazily (-24° ↔ +52°) as it sways down. Unmistakably snow, though at high counts all-spokes in the far plane can feel slightly busy.

B · Depth-Layered Hybrid

Recommended
Mock B · Foreground Glyphs + Bokeh Depth 3-plane parallax

What it does: Combines 3 depth-of-field planes: Near (25%): hero 6-spoke icon glyphs (r = 4.5–7px, subtle dark rim-shadow so they pop on pale day snow skies) tumbling & swaying wide; Mid (40%): smaller 6-spoke glyphs (r = 2.6–4px); Far (35%): soft out-of-focus snow powder bokeh drifting slowly behind the clouds.

C · Branched Crystal + Icon Glyphs

Dendritic Hero Flakes
Mock C · Dendrite chevrons on near flakes 6-spoke + V-ticks

What it does: Builds on Mock B's 3-plane depth, adding short 60° chevron side-branches at 62% of each spoke on the largest foreground flakes (r > 5.2px), while mid-plane flakes keep the clean 6-spoke icon silhouette.

1b · WMO Snow Sub-Variants in the Sky (Mirroring the Icon Ladder)

The icon painter already distinguishes Light Snow (71), Heavy Snow (75), Snow Grains (77), and Snow Showers (85/86), but sky.js currently paints the exact same generic snow scene for all of them. Here is how the sky simulation responds to the WMO variant:

WMO 71 · Light Flurries

Sparse · high buoyancy 71

~28 flakes, slower terminal velocity, wider pendular float and gentle updraft pauses.

WMO 75 · Heavy / Blizzard

Wind-slanted · blowing wisps 75

110 multi-plane 6-spoke flakes driven at a diagonal slant by periodic wind gusts + blowing snow wisps.

WMO 77 · Snow Grains

Fast opaque mini-pellets 77

Matches painter.js snowGrains: small opaque white ice grains (r = 1.4–2.1px) falling fast and straight without spoke rotation.

WMO 85/86 · Snow Showers

Peeking sun/moon + snow 85

Matches showersPeek(): sun or moon shines through a broken cloud deck while 6-spoke flakes tumble through the light shafts.

2 · All Other Weather Conditions — Current vs. Improved Mocks Full Scene Suite

Side-by-side comparison of every other weather scene in sky.js. Left is the exact current algorithm; right is the proposed upgrade using Cairo-ready primitives (linear/radial gradients, polylines, sprite tinting, and multi-plane parallax).

Thunderstorm (WMO 95 / CAPE ≥ 1200)

High Impact
Current · Fixed 3-seg line
Improved · Forked bolts + Cloud flash 95

Current issue: drawLightning() strokes the exact same 3-segment yellow line at x = 50% every 3.1s; clouds stay flat white blobs.
Improved: Procedural multi-branch forked bolts at varied horizontal strike points (gold outer glow + white-hot core), in-cloud sheet lightning illuminating the dark storm anvil bellies from within, and wind-lashed rain curtains.

Severe Thunderstorm with Hail (WMO 96 / 99)

Bug + Upgrade
Current · No lightning in hail!
Improved · Bolts + Glinting Ice Stones 96/99

Current issue: WMO 96 & 99 are Thunderstorm with hail, and the icon shows a bolt + hailstones — yet SCENE_FEATURES.hail in sky.js omits bolt: 1, so hailstorms have zero lightning! Stones are also plain unlit circles.
Improved: Restores forked lightning + cloud flashes, and upgrades hailstones to match #r-hail: icy blue-white spheres (#eaf6ff, #9fc9ee rim, specular highlight dot) that bounce, scatter, and shatter into micro-chips along the bottom floor.

Fog & Rime Fog (WMO 45 / 48)

High Impact
Current · Flat cutout hills
Improved · Interleaved valley mist + Rime 45/48

Current issue: drawFogHills() fills two flat, hard-edged sine polygons, and all fog puffs drift exclusively in front of both hills — making the ridges look like cardboard cutouts.
Improved: Interleaves a mid-valley mist layer between the far and near ridges so the ridges separate in depth; adds a veiled, diffused sun/moon glow bleeding through upper haze; softens ridge bases; and for Rime Fog (48) suspends twinkling 4-point frost sparkles (#spark) in the cold air.

Rain, Drizzle & Showers (WMO 51–65, 80–82)

Depth & Curtains
Current · Uniform alpha lines
Improved · Tapered drops + Splash mist 63/82

Current issue: 90 single-width lines at flat 0.45 alpha with no depth separation, no rain shafts, and no floor interaction.
Improved: 3-plane depth parallax with gradient-tapered streaks (fading tail → bright teardrop-bulb head with catch-light), sweeping diagonal rain shafts (virga curtains) drifting from the dark cloud bases, and subtle splash ripples / rising mist at the card foot.

Sleet & Freezing Rain (WMO 56–57, 66–67)

Mixed Phase
Current · Lines + white dots
Improved · Drops + 6-spoke Flakes + Glaze 66/67

Current issue: Mixes flat lines with plain white dots — neither phase looks distinct, and freezing rain's signature glaze is absent.
Improved: Pairs slanted aqua/navy rain streaks with tumbling 6-spoke icon snowflakes (#flake) and fast-falling translucent ice pellets, plus a subtle crystalline ice-glaze shimmer along the bottom edge (mirroring the glaze underline in painter.js sceneSleet).

Wind / Gale (wind & windy)

Icon-Matched
Current · Rigid horizontal lines
Improved · Curved ribbons + Eddies Wind

Current issue: drawWindStreaks() draws 22 dead-straight horizontal line segments (moveTo(x,y); lineTo(x+L,y)) that read like CRT scanlines.
Improved: Flowing sine-curved aerodynamic ribbons with tapered head/tail alpha gradients, occasional curling vortex eddies that echo the looped swoosh of #r-wind / painter.js sceneWind, and fast-scudding translucent cloud wisps.

Overcast & Partly Cloudy (WMO 2 / 3)

2-Tone Cloud Deck
Current · Flat white puffs
Improved · Shaded bellies + Rear bank 02/03

Current issue: makeCloudSprite() is a pure-white radial blob stamped 5 times; in Overcast, white blobs on a grey sky flatten out with no cloud-base shadow.
Improved: Mirrors the two-bank Overcast icon (#r-overcast-day): a darker slate-blue rear stratus deck behind two-tone cumulus sprites (warm/bright crown, cool shadowed underbelly), with silver-lining rim glow when passing near the sun or moon.

Clear Sky — Sunbeams & Night Meteor (WMO 0 / 1)

Celestial Polish
Current · Sun / Moon + Stars
Improved · Crepuscular shafts / Meteors 00/01

Current: Rotating sun rays by day; moon + 70 stars by night.
Improved: By day, adds broad, ultra-soft diagonal crepuscular light shafts and subtle warm atmospheric motes; by night (toggle Night in the top bar), adds a faint Milky Way dust band, 4-point cyan/white #spark flares, and an occasional shooting star streaking softly across the upper sky.

4 · Cairo Porting Notes (extension/sky.js & app/src/sky.js)

Every effect on this page uses only standard 2D Cairo equivalents (cr.moveTo/lineTo/curveTo, Cairo.LinearGradient, Cairo.RadialGradient, and pre-rendered Cairo.ImageSurface sprites) so it drops cleanly into both the GNOME Shell extension and the GTK4 app's half-res FX plane:

Scene / Feature Current sky.js Improved Cairo Implementation
Snow (71, 73, 75, 77, 85/86) 70 white circles (cr.arc, r = 1.8–3.6), single-frequency sine sway. 3-tier depth field: Near & mid tiers draw the 6-spoke painter.js flake (or blit a 24×24 pre-rendered 6-spoke sprite rotated by cr.rotate(rot) for zero per-flake stroke overhead!), rocking lazily (sin(t * 1.1 + ph) * 0.65 rad). Far tier draws soft radial powder puffs. Variant grains (77) draws fast vertical mini-pellets; showers (85/86) enables sun/moon behind broken clouds.
Thunderstorm (95) & Hail (96/99) Fixed 3-segment polyline at x = 0.5w; hail scene lacks bolt: 1; hailstones are flat white circles. Add bolt: 1 to SCENE_FEATURES.hail. Reseed bolt strike x and 4–5 jittered trunk vertices + 2 branch forks per 3.1s cycle; stroke once at 7*scale with amber alpha 0.32 (glow) and once at 2.6*scale in white-gold. Paint a radial cloud-belly flash above the strike point. Give hailstones an #9fc9ee rim + upper-left specular highlight and 2–3 micro-splinter particles on floor bounce.
Fog (45) & Rime Fog (48) 2 flat-filled sine ridges; all 15 fog ovals painted in front of both ridges. Split sky.fog into mid-valley puffs (drawn between ridge 0 and ridge 1 at y = 0.28..0.46h) and foreground puffs (drawn in front of ridge 1). Fill ridges with a vertical LinearGradient that dissolves their feet into the mist wash. When variant === 'rime' (code 48), draw 14 slowly drifting 4-point sparkle() frost glints.
Rain (51–65, 80–82) & Sleet (56–67) Flat-alpha 0.45 line strokes; sleet mixes lines with plain circles. 3 depth planes of rain streaks with tapered alpha (or teardrop-bulb leading tip on near drops), plus 3–4 wide diagonal rain-curtain bands (LinearGradient at 16° slant) and faint elliptical floor splash rings. In sleet, replace circle flakes with tumbling 6-spoke icon flakes + glinting ice pellets.
Wind (wind) & Clouds (cloud) Straight horizontal segments; single white puff cloud sprite. Draw wind streaks as sine-curved cubic Beziers with tapered dash/gradient ends, plus 3 travelling loop-curl eddies (painter.js sceneWind geometry). Add a shaded underbelly stop to makeCloudSprite() and a darker rear stratus layer for cloud/storm.
